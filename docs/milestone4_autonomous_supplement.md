# Milestone 4 autonomous scheduling supplement — 2026-10-05

This is a new grant linked to the closed Milestone 4 campaign and limited M5A
checkpoint. Historical STOP/NO_GO decisions and charges remain preserved.
Milestone 5 stays open. The stable `controller.gvs_nmpc@7.0.0` path is used,
with default CasADi functions and no experimental native provider or observer.

One shared research-model context chooses a control search, structure search,
bounded retained-evidence diagnosis, or voluntary stop. The new native
`research.decide` interface embeds the existing `SearchBatchPlan`. It reuses
source verification, parameter-domain validation, candidate reconstruction,
ask/tell, full backend evaluation, profiling, comparisons and receipt accounting.
Historical stage-prescribed experiments remain unchanged and reproducible.

The primary case is the verified `.10/.05` holding/terminal result beginning
`110dc2c9`, near/far `.16/.11 m`, section scale `.95`, compliant numerical
material. It failed holding speed acceptance. The known passing `.05/.05`
incumbent `91c3ba1b01d6499fb26df8f95409401b` and all comparable history are
honestly supplied. The question is whether further bounded control/structural
evidence is useful for understanding that acceptance loss. No fallback is
selected. A legitimate early stop is retained as incomplete multibatch coverage.

Frozen aggregate ceilings: 24 provider attempts, 60 workflow calls, 4 full
backend attempts, 9,000 charged seconds, 12 standalone numerical operations,
zero workers/subagents. Each batch has at most two backend attempts, preserving
capacity for feedback and delivery. Complete execution reservations are the
shared 900-second simulation, 30-second evaluation, 60-second profile, five
seconds preparation, plus 600 seconds/four provider/four workflow protected
interpretation and planning capacity. These are reservations, not actual costs.
Unused ceilings are not targets. No M5 protected allocation is transferred.

Protocol corrections are cumulative: four total, two consecutive maximum,
including rejected business inputs; successful valid calls reset consecutive
only. One narrow live engineering repair is allowed within the same ledger.
Completed backend results must never be replayed to repair interpretation/export.

Both speed weights and the existing near/far length, section-scale and material
scenario domains remain available when permissions, prerequisites and capacity
allow. The serialized native schema narrows its action enum to current legal
actions. Restrictions have concrete reasons. A changed structure triggers
necessary reconstruction; subsequent control search is optional. The planning
projection restores research policy from execution children without changing
their science. Only existing retained prediction/plans/motion diagnostic queries
are exposed here; local numerical comparisons lack bound adoption/horizon
prerequisites and report that gap. Duplicate queries return prior evidence
unless the model supplies a replication justification.

Every decision receives verified history, exact latest feedback and F aliases,
legal capabilities, remaining budgets and predecessor identity. Each later plan
must cite current actual feedback. Provider requests/responses, resolved calls,
rejections, candidate/evidence receipts, feedback order and stop decisions are
retained. Referencing evidence validates provenance, not scientific causality.

Acceptance separates implementation checks, real execution, feedback coverage,
physical improvement and realtime. Core coverage requires two new executed
search batches within one run, each with a new full evaluation, the second
plan citing actual first-batch evidence, model-authored reasoning and a grounded
final decision. Offline fixtures and old results do not count.

Evidence is exported to `evidence/milestone4_autonomous_20261005`. Live results
and the final acceptance audit will be added after execution.
