# Native research successor v3, 2026-10-07

This is the explicitly authorized successor to the sealed v2 failure. It creates
one new cumulative ledger and reuses shared context, selected history, native
schemas, candidate construction, stable v7 and authoritative joint acceptance.
Old activity, answers, grants, costs and protected formal allocations remain sealed.

The research question is unchanged: can design/control adaptation improve joint
reach-and-hold under the existing five small initial-state variations? Development
observations are not unseen tests. A structural/control effect needs same-condition
reference evidence, or an allocated fresh reference, within eight research attempts.
The full eight-variable pool remains available. The model authors hypotheses,
subsets, methods, prospective case choices, interpretations and subsequent actions.
Explicit points are model proposals, not numerical optimization. Installed bounded
coordinate ask/tell generates numeric proposals; categorical choices require explicit
enumeration. Retained affine/prediction evidence cannot guarantee nonlinear reach,
holding or robustness and cannot screen candidates under sealed M5 limitations.
No forced structural edit, minimum round count or successful candidate is required.

`conditional_verification@3.0.0` protects twenty backend-count slots from search.
It does not deduct their entire 19,800-second/60-operation future schedule during
research. Each batch, at most two attempts, reserves its complete execution plus
feedback/delivery; the Host protects at least two requests, ten operations and
1,200 seconds for delivery. The entire eligible-candidate comparison (20 fresh
attempts, 60 receipt operations, 19,800 seconds) plus delivery must fit remaining
count, request, workflow, charged-time and elapsed-time budgets before it starts.
Otherwise it remains not started, with no reduced post-outcome schedule. This
trades guaranteed final verification for executable bounded development.

Candidate eligibility/order are frozen before live work: effective incumbent-relative
design/control change, fresh complete bound nominal joint pass; first nondominated
eligible result in native completed-search order. Freeze exact configuration and full
changes before verification. Five cases in existing order, seeds17/18, incumbent then
candidate for every slot. No cached/search substitutions, success retries, tuning or
midway replacement. Without a candidate, ten incumbent-only slots answer its still
unresolved perturbation performance if the full ten-slot schedule plus delivery fits.
Incomplete evidence suppresses improvement; deterministic development coverage does
not establish population reliability or LLM superiority over the mathematical baseline.

Before every request, the current campaign/session owns a versioned immutable authority
snapshot. Complete payload checks compare visible menu, budget and schema against that
snapshot. Dispatch recomputes actual capacity; changes during reasoning produce current
authority feedback rather than stale-menu formatting corrections. Historical archive
scope remains separate. Current working-state and archive manifest are adopted together.
This also covers schema corrections and permitted transport continuation. A final-report
request can only stop and cannot reopen the scientific trajectory.

Hard maxima are 28 backends (8 research,20 verification),24 provider attempts including
failures/retries/corrections,200 workflow operations,16 supported standalone numerical
operations,36,000 charged seconds and zero extra experimental roles/workers. Preparation
has a two-hour active engineering cap. Its chargeable work counts in the same totals.
`successor_clock@3.0.0` persists one ten-hour live origin immediately before the first
real operation after authorized preflight. Authorization waits are separately recorded;
subsequent waits/restarts never pause or reset the live clock. This does not retroactively
change v2. At most four protocol corrections/two consecutive, two transport retries/one
per failed request and two material live repairs. Completed backend work is never replayed.
Recurring material defects, hard limits or scientific STOP close the affected trajectory.

Targeted preflight uses actual native payload/dispatch/restore paths and saved records,
with zero provider/backend activity. Results and any preparation failures are retained.
Provider, endpoint, model, reasoning, token limits, credential loader and TLS remain
configured as before. The duration (.35s), control period (.01s), physics step (.0005s),
inclusive final .05s holding window, .01m position and .02m/s speed criteria, input bounds,
backend and solver stopping are unchanged. Experimental v8 and formal three groups are excluded.

Entry: `python examples/research_campaign_v3.py prepare`, then `live` on the same prepared
ledger after the tested implementation and policy are committed and sealed. Existing or
attempted campaigns reject reset. Progress is normally pushed to `origin/feat/gvs-dynamics`.
The observed `recover-live` mode is restricted to the two saved settled engineering
defects; it preserves the original ledger/clock and never replays completed executions.
It is no longer usable after its two repair slots have been consumed.
