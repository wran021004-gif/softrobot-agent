PPO/NMPC/Native active control, initialization, dynamic Replay and SELECT are not advertised or admitted for this mode. Generate/propose, diagnose, mathematical research, evidence access and legal STOP remain available.

The original TaskDefinition still retains composition scaffolding for compatibility; its Reach goal/controller is not projected as a passive objective or executable protocol. Its retained grid is explicitly scoped as `static_load_cases`, not evidence of dynamic acceptance.
6. Existing `SOLVER` is the one versioned passive-kernel settings source. Each model receives a copy; results record the actual effective settings, including Hessian/active-set/KKT tolerances, SLSQP/QP limits and native options. No task/Study JSON copies or new tuning switches were introduced. Historical numerical records were not changed.

Schema validity does not waive candidate ownership, saved-source availability, scalar/dimension checks, actual equilibrium qualification or remaining budget. Those original admission checks still produce explicit preparation/not-applicable responses.
