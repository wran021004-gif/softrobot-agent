are unchanged. No real Provider requests or new physical executions in C0.

TaskAdapter projects frozen evaluator bounds into signed upper/lower violations,
with load, window, signal, unit and source pointer. New acceptance records carry
these derived observations; legacy records use a read-only compatibility view.
Positive absolute frozen bounds supply normalization. Zero bounds stay unscaled:
in particular a no-contact condition does not acquire an arbitrary epsilon score.
Incomplete/invalid observations cannot acquire a comparable TaskGap.

compiler-compatible alias. Store read snapshots exist only during a request's
preparation. Immutable artifacts are still hash checked and returned detached.
Short reference enumeration and confirmed-delivery parsing share that scope.

default L0 has one owner for repeated task-gap facts. Reporter is not required
for deterministic numerical delivery or certificate admission.
