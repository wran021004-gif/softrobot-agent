There are **39 canonical Main-visible IDs**: 21 active, 24 passive, with six shared geometry IDs. Two additional registry entries are hidden historical compatibility names. Exposure means the complete Schema/compiler/Worker/result path is present; it does not bypass frozen method grants, source admission, FamilySpace bounds, model qualification, or budget.

## Matrix legend

- **F — Fully exposed and executable** within ordinary candidate/method/state/input admission. This is a numerical observation, never physical task acceptance.
- **C — Conditionally available**: nonzero geometric rows/velocity, qualified equilibrium, LICQ/strict complementarity, or declared projection are additional conditions. Unmet conditions return a specific not-applicable result.
- **I — Internal-only / historical compatibility**: absent from new executable V4 quantity enums; historical requests/read views retain their original meaning.
- **U — Unsupported / not implemented**: listed separately below; absent from executable enums.

Operation letters are inspect/differentiate/optimize/compare. D/O additionally require explicit Main goal, one or two effective authorized continuous parameters, a finite cap and a scalar target. They produce checked two-scale local secants/bounded proposals, without a strict derivative, global optimum or task-success certificate. Active-set/basis/cone-only quantities intentionally offer inspect/compare only.

**A**: exact contact-free named-hinge MuJoCo state, legal ordered absolute SI tensions, input bounds and frozen mounting/forces. **P**: contact-free fixed-payout assembly (`model.nu=0`), actual named frozen load, material station, mounting/gravity and unloaded reference; payout is fixed across load cases. Smooth tangent matrices require both physical qualification and the declared regularity conditions. `nv` is named hinge count, `ntendon` is physical tendon count, `nu` is active tension-channel count, `free` is tangent dimension, and `projection` is the number of explicit orthonormal world output rows.

**Sources**: N = `extensions.tendon_family.mujoco_analysis.NativeModel`; P = `extensions.tendon_family.passive_equilibrium`, except `P.row_match` in `passive_inverse`; R = `mechanism_registry`. Native material-point export reuses the original material-station resolver and `mj_jac`.

**Evidence**: S25-C = `test_stage25_architecture::test_registry_schema_compiler_and_physical_admission` checks all 24 passive and 21 active advertised IDs through actual compilation, mode filtering, goal and operation admission. S25-N = `test_real_native_coverage_and_unilateral_projection` derives all canonical passive observations from one real loaded state and verifies the active material-point/force exports. ## PHS source comparison and unavailable capabilities

The source `passive_inverse.matching()` combines useful all-tendon row geometry with a **four piecewise-curvature group prototype** and horizontal tip constraints. Only general row matching is reused; V4 explicitly binds arbitrary world output directions/subspaces and separately identifies positive constraints. The eight-coordinate curvature prototype is **I/U for general V4 research**, not a general 3D solver.

The source `modal_target()` constructs ideal horizontal tip-null modes, monitor-y excursions, and quasistatic cone responses. It is not a dynamic eigenmode or frequency-response backend. Its general unilateral QP component is already available through `tangent`; its task-specific ideal-mode search was not migrated. Source `design_sensitivity` and `task_design_sensitivity` supply the regular KKT theory used by Stage II; II.5 keeps that kernel and organizes its bindings/results.

| Capability | Status / precise gap |
|---|---|
| General native dynamic eigenmodes, frequency response | U: no registered native mechanism backend. A compiled mass/stiffness matrix alone does not provide this experiment. |
| Integrated energy/dissipation mechanism | U: stiffness and damping parameters are exposed, but there is no integrated energy/dissipation target backend. |
| Hybrid active/passive, contact sensitivity | U: not implemented; no silent friction/contact or zero-active-force substitution. |
| Arbitrary physical coordinate frames | U beyond world and named hinge/tendon coordinates; only explicit world directions/orthonormal projections are currently bound. |
| Multi-load optimization/aggregate gradients | U as a new aggregate optimizer. Current compare evaluates selected frozen loads on one model and may deterministically take a scalar minimum/maximum. Local structural directions use a scientifically selected single load and the original worker. |
| Passive finite-time independent Replay/evaluator/SELECT | U: not registered. Static diagnostic bounds never become task acceptance. |

## Cross-task contract

The same physics functions accept different legal FamilySpace parameters, material stations, world directions/projection planes and named frozen load conditions. The real comparison changes the load from world-y to world-z on the identical one-tendon assembly, reuses its unloaded solution, and records three qualified cases and a deterministic maximum. Material point lower bounds, tip projection upper bounds and a non-divided tip-minus-scaled-material diagnostic are evaluated independently.

This supports reusable constrained mechanism research. It does not establish contact/hardware validity, arbitrary local frames, complete passive dynamic goals, a multi-case optimizer, or legal startup of a full autonomous passive Study.
