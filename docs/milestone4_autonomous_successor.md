# Milestone 4 autonomous research successor — 2026-10-06

This separate campaign links the stopped 2026-10-05 supplement. Its unused grant
is not reopened or transferred. M5A is complete; M5 stays open with its protected
allocations untouched. The original bounded-flow M4 closure is preserved.

Shared chronology now comes from Store reservation/completion events. The legacy
`latest_execution` means the last attempted simulation, including incomplete or
failed attempts. `latest_completed_evaluation` means the last official evaluation
completion; it implies neither acceptance nor a profile. `latest_complete_result`
requires retained full profiled facts. Research context and delivery use the same
rule. Imported historical records have no place in the new campaign's local event
sequence; missing chronology is reported instead of guessed. Reversing the saved
.075/.09 result presentation still identifies .09 as last and preserves selection.

`research.decide` now separates evidence-bound observations, model interpretations
with support/contradictions/scope/uncertainty, and the action/rationale. The small
deterministic check covers copied factual values and explicit differences or
comparisons. It rejects a wrong value with the expected evidence-derived value,
without prescribing a hypothesis or route. References do not prove causality.
Voluntary stop under unresolved uncertainty remains legal and seals that case.
No additional model judge or interpretation request of the predecessor is used.

The successor primary asks which affordable evidence could distinguish response
differences on the shortened compliant structure from solver-plan selection,
weight interaction or structural sensitivity. Saved .075/.09/.10 holding values
all fail speed while their maxima decrease; both 0/.05 and .05/.05 pass. These
counterexamples invalidate a claimed narrow passing window established by the
old sparse samples. The question concerns the value of discriminating evidence,
not merely finding the already-known passing incumbent.

versus joint .05/.05 success, asking about interaction or structure dependence.

coverage and capacity for two complete executions plus shared delivery. It uses
the same Host and cumulative ledger/recovery counters, seals the primary, and
never reopens it. Two cases' batches cannot be combined to pass coverage.

The new aggregate grant is 24 provider / 60 workflow / 4 backend / 9,000 charged
seconds / 12 standalone diagnostic operations / zero workers or subagents.

reservations, four cumulative corrections/two consecutive, and one narrow live

Searches normally reuse known configurations. An explicit `replication_reason`
permits a new backend execution of a known point, identified separately from a
novel configuration; a batch holds one execution per scientific configuration.
Repetition does not expand domains or change scoring and acceptance.

linked predecessor chronology/claim correction without changing its original

inside one case, with the second plan explicitly using actual first-batch feedback,
model explanation and a grounded final decision; legitimate early stopping is
reported as incomplete coverage. Physical superiority and realtime are separate.
