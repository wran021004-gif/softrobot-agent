# Stage 3.41 deterministic control evidence

The evidence interfaces use immutable simulation export manifests in the existing SQLite artifact store. Inputs are execution IDs, update indices and snapshot IDs; the public CLI resolves local storage through `evidence/control_evidence_catalog.json`. No diagnostic LLM, worker, external search, actuator travel optimization or tolerance change is introduced.

## Reproduction

Use the existing `softagent` interpreter. These commands inspect saved evidence without a solver or provider request:

```powershell
$env:OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS='1'
$env:OMP_NUM_THREADS='1'
$env:MKL_NUM_THREADS='1'
& 'C:/Users/gugugaga/miniconda3/envs/softagent/python.exe' examples/control_evidence.py prediction --execution f9b8c232a576465ab5ef1ca92658faad --updates 0 4 34
& 'C:/Users/gugugaga/miniconda3/envs/softagent/python.exe' examples/control_evidence.py plans --execution 199e9dd6528f4b39963d9571598d290a --updates 0 4 34
& 'C:/Users/gugugaga/miniconda3/envs/softagent/python.exe' examples/control_evidence.py compare --execution f9b8c232a576465ab5ef1ca92658faad --variant 199e9dd6528f4b39963d9571598d290a --changed-factor recording
```

`ControlEvidence.query(EvidenceQuery(...))` and `ControlEvidence.compare(ExecutionComparison(...))` are the typed Python entrypoints. Registered tools are `control.inspect_evidence@1.0.0` and `control.compare_evidence@1.0.0`; they perform no solves. The schema reports ownership, configuration and execution identities, update/time selection, observations with units, references, missing data and comparison conditions. Full arrays remain in referenced backend artifacts.

The separately invoked `local-compare` operation uses `LocalComparisonRequest`, `freeze_comparison` and `run_comparison`. It writes its acceptance protocol before two local solves, with durable counters capped at four solves and 1,200 seconds across this study. It refuses to repeat a snapshot comparison. The completed invocation was:

```powershell
& 'C:/Users/gugugaga/miniconda3/envs/softagent/python.exe' examples/control_evidence.py local-compare --execution 199e9dd6528f4b39963d9571598d290a --snapshot a58d1c80fb8192a78cc3bcdd0bc8fc5e90d9e3e12890adf463dad620caa50a0e
```

This is provenance, not permission to repeat a completed experiment. The one-shot recording/validation and live-session entrypoints also refuse repeated attempts. Missing historical horizons are never regenerated by inspection.

## Interpretation and recording

Stage 3.40 selected 26 noninitialization plans and 9 initialization plans. Its official reach error was 18.349652 mm against 10 mm. The final accepted plan itself predicted 15.930070 mm at the actual deadline, while the actual next-state tip differed from prediction by 3.308744 mm. These norms are not additive error components. The recorded evidence therefore supports both insufficient predicted deadline progress and prediction discrepancy, without identifying a single dominant cause.

The new fixed-design recording replay retains bounded snapshots at updates 0, 4 and 34, including complete prepared and pre-preparation warm horizons, initialization/selected/returned plans, bounded iterate checkpoints, independently recomputed residual/objective facts, motion predictions, selection ownership and timing. Snapshot IDs identify content before capture timing is appended; the enclosing immutable artifact hash binds the complete record. Backend state, physics, control parameters and trajectory references are supplied by the same export manifest.

Numeric plan metrics and objective components were recomputed during recording and then sealed. Subsequent inspection only summarizes those recorded quantities. Feasibility uses the unchanged maximum scaled residual of `1e-5`: kinematic residuals are divided by `10 rad/m`; generalized-force residuals by `0.001 N*m^2/rad`; initial-state residuals by their declared decision-state scales; tension bounds use N. These definitions remain separate from position cost normalization and official reach acceptance.

The recording replay used the exact Stage 3.40 design and scientific conditions. Its actual-command artifact is byte-identical to Stage 3.40, and its terminal position matches; this does not imply that timed instrumentation guarantees historical reproduction. Direct measured callback, bounded verification and capture costs total about 0.316 seconds over three snapshots. Warm-horizon copying and serialization were not separately timed. Complete-update timing includes operational recording work; cross-run timing differences are not an overhead estimate.

The recording's post-run report was not produced: a source-hash mismatch was first resolved by restoring the exact frozen source bytes; the subsequent report request could not reserve 1,500 seconds against 1,379.75 seconds remaining. Both rejections remain in the source ledger. No grant was reset or enlarged, no replacement recording occurred, and read-only array summaries are not labeled an official replay evaluation. The corrected runner reserves enough reporting capacity for future separately authorized studies without modifying this recorded grant.

`diagnosis.json` records the final supported interpretation, alternatives, adoption decision, fixed-design outcome and next discriminating comparison. `local_comparison.json` preserves the predeclared shared-metric gate and both variants. `validation_result.json` contains the separate official fixed-design evaluation. The fresh model-authored session, when completed, is reported separately in the `live_*` files; protocol completion never implies reach success.

Large raw numerical plans, backend traces, ledgers and provider payloads stay under the recorded local artifact paths. `raw_artifacts.json` and `live_raw_artifacts.json` list sizes and SHA-256 hashes. The Git-byte manifest is generated and verified with `examples/verify_git_blob_manifest.py`; it excludes itself. No credentials are exported.
