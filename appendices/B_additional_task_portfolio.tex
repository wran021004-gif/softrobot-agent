\section{Task Portfolio and Reporting Conventions}
\label{app:task-portfolio}

The archived FULL portfolio contains T01--T10 and PHS-2D-7S. Their required-condition counts sum to 30. T08 is a development-valid passive-response case and is not treated as a registered benchmark task. T10 includes two additional frozen controls, which are reported separately. PHS-2D-9S is a separate independent study and is not included in the eleven-task FULL denominator.

The task-specific metrics are not collapsed into a single cross-task raw-error average. A normalized violation may be used within a task when its normalization is defined by the frozen task contract. Condition-level values and the worst required condition remain available in the source data.

\subsection{PHS-2D study identities}

PHS-2D-7S is the seven-segment, four-tendon passive case included in the eleven-task FULL portfolio. Its ten required conditions pass; its minimum loaded-point displacement is 14.798 mm, maximum projected tip displacement is 4.347 mm, and maximum tip-to-point ratio is 0.15375.

PHS-2D-9S is an independent nine-segment, four-tendon study with eight massive guide plates and no active actuators. Its ten prescribed signed-load evaluations pass; its minimum loaded-point displacement is 11.155 mm, maximum projected tip displacement is 3.068 mm, and maximum tip-to-point ratio is 0.1093. These two studies are reported separately and their structural parameters and evaluation records are not combined.
