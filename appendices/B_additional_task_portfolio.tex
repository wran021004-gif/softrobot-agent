\section{Additional Task Portfolio}
\label{app:portfolio}

The task portfolio spans several physical problem classes. The table below provides a compact index; detailed parameters and raw execution traces are maintained with the corresponding task records.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\caption{Additional task families.}
\label{tab:additional-tasks}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}l Y@{}}
\toprule
Task & Capability represented \\
\midrule
M2 & Loaded reach-and-hold under an external payload. \\
M3 & Fast reach-and-stop with a short terminal holding window. \\
M4 & Timed polyline trajectory tracking. \\
M5 & Position and tool-axis pointing. \\
M6 & Loaded position and tool-axis scanning. \\
C1 & Reduced-channel actuation with four independent tendon channels. \\
Figure-eight & Periodic trajectory tracking with a four-tendon structure. \\
PHS-2D & Passive horizontal tip stabilization under multiple loading conditions. \\
P3 & Passive progressive stiffness under bilateral multi-stage loading. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

The portfolio is not intended to imply that all tasks share the same acceptance metric. Reach-and-hold uses position and velocity constraints; tracking uses time-indexed trajectory error; tool-axis tasks use orientation-direction error; passive tasks use load--displacement response, stiffness progression, recruitment, recovery, and contact constraints.
