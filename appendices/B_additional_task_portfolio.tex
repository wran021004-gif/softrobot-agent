The independent PHS-2D-9S Study is not counted again in the portfolio. Its final nine-segment, four-passive-tendon design passes ten signed-load cases, with minimum force-point motion 11.155~mm, maximum projected tip displacement 3.068~mm, and maximum tip-to-point ratio 0.1093. The full ten-condition measurements, centerlines, tendon tensions, active tendon sets, and numerical confirmations are supplied with the research evidence package.

For T01--T03 and T10, the terminal and holding metrics refer to their original evaluation windows. T05--T06 retain directed tool-axis measurements rather than treating orientation as an unsigned axis alignment. T07 shares a structural design across all required conditions while permitting condition-specific controls. Passive studies use fixed payout and prescribed loads; they do not substitute an active controller for a passive restraint.
