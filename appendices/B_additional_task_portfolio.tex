\section{Task Portfolio and Reporting Conventions}
\label{app:task-portfolio}

The FULL portfolio comprises T01--T10 and PHS-2D-7S. The required-condition counts sum to 30. T10 includes two additional frozen controls, which are reported separately. The independent PHS-2D-9S mechanism study has its own ten-condition evaluation and is not included in the FULL denominator.
