\section{Resource accounting}
\label{app:cross-model-profile}

Figure~\ref{fig:knowledge-skill-resources} and Table~\ref{tab:resource-ablation} aggregate the four Knowledge/Skill configurations over the same eleven tasks. Mathematical analyses count research-analysis operations, and physical evaluations count physical-evaluation operations. Core requests count the recorded requests for each run. Token consumption follows the cumulative token totals in the source workbook. The four resources retain their own units and are summed separately.

The totals are obtained from the 44 task-result records in the author-provided Knowledge/Skill task-comparison workbook and reconciled against its four configuration-summary rows.
