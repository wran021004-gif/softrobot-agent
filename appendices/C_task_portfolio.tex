\section{Task Portfolio and Physical Metrics}
\label{app:task-portfolio}

The FULL portfolio comprises the eleven tasks shown in Fig.~\ref{fig:task-portfolio}. The required-condition counts sum to 30. Impact Recovery includes two additional frozen-input controls, which are reported separately. Passive Tip Suppression contributes ten signed-loading conditions to the benchmark. Table~\ref{tab:task-metrics-appendix} defines the task-specific quantities and reporting conventions; Table~\ref{tab:physical-portfolio} gives the corresponding FULL outcomes.
