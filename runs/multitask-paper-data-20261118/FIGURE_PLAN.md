**Figure_A_structure_evolution**

Native MuJoCo views of PHS-2D and T07. Each task uses a fixed camera and scale across its selected structures. PHS F0 is an initial diagnostic, not a registered accepted design. Only the two T07 morphologies with original physical XML are shown; its other math-only candidates remain in the evidence table. Previously completed visual mounting, anchor and junction envelopes are retained as visual-only elements.

**Figure_B_research_trajectories**

Archived evidence supports route changes in T04 and T10. T04 switches from an incomplete GVS route to native bank/feedforward execution. T10 retains the same morphology while changing controller construction. Diagnostic values with different protocol scopes are discrete comparisons, not a convergence curve. Final T10 common-recipe cases and frozen-input controls remain separate.

**Figure_C_physical_outcomes**

Recorded native states of T09 show approach, maximum travel and holding; plots use actual button displacement and contact force in the specified hold window. T04 tracking and T10 feedback controls are available as separate execution alternatives. Videos are rendered directly from archived states; camera, source time, force cues and playback rates are documented outside the image.
