# Source and claim boundaries for manuscript v0.3

- The paper treats Softrobot-Agent as one integrated research system; it does not organize the narrative around who initiated an individual design action.
- C1/C2/C3 are the manuscript's scientific contributions.
- KKT, the envelope theorem, implicit differentiation, sensitivity analysis, and four-point interaction are established mathematical tools. The contribution is their organization into task-conditioned, agent-selectable research operations with explicit validity checks.
- Research Intent Projection is a central architectural concept. The exact implementation boundary must be checked in the current `scientific_question_v4` path before claiming a completed, independently ablatable module.
- No-Math and Fixed-Plan are different interventions.
- No-Math and No-Pairwise-Interaction are nested, not independent factorial conditions.
- No-Knowledge requires alignment of historical and current knowledge assets.
- A 200-request budget is a hard cap, not a proof of design-space infeasibility.
- The paper does not claim calibrated Bayesian VOI, globally valid structural gradients, universal descent certificates, or hardware validation without corresponding evidence.
- Experimental figures must be the original exported files. This project contains no synthetic experimental plot assets.
