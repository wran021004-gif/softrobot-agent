# Figure assets and provenance

The approved architecture figure is sourced from fig01_research_framework.tex and is frozen. Do not edit or replace it.

The older exported PDF files in this directory belong to the v0.4 manuscript workflow. They are retained for provenance and must not be inserted into the current manuscript without checking task identity, structure, and metrics.

The current quantitative figure data are in paper_handoff/figure_data/, with specifications in paper_handoff/figure_manifest.md and the manuscript-level plan in PAPER_FIGURE_TABLE_PLAN.md. The publication palette and typography are defined in PAPER_STYLE_GUIDE.md.

Quantitative plots are generated from the normalized handoff CSVs. Figure 3 additionally requires MuJoCo-native rendering of the four actual PHS-2D-9S candidate models at a common camera and scale. Do not substitute conceptual drawings or seven-segment models for those renders.
