# Experimental matrix — manuscript v1.1

## Completed FULL portfolio

Eleven completed task cases: T01–T10 and the nine-segment, four-tendon PHS-2D Round-3 case. The archived summary contains 30 passed required conditions among 30 required records. Two additional T10 frozen controls are reported separately. This is a completed-case portfolio, not a multi-seed success-rate estimate.

## Research-mode comparison

The first comparison batch contains PHS-2D, T01, T04, and T07, with one A and one B run per task; C is the corresponding historical FULL reference. A is LLM-only physical-feedback design. B adds direct bottom-level mathematical tools but not the high-level research-intent projection and mathematical orchestration. C is the complete FULL research process.

The paper presents representative A/B/C task-wise outcomes for T01, T04, and T07. PHS-2D is presented as the principal nine-segment mathematical and passive-design case using its independent Round-3 evidence package.

## Component evidence

The FULL traces contain direct evidence of:
- Agent hypotheses and research-route changes in T01, T02, T04, T07, T10, and PHS-2D;
- actual Knowledge reads in T01, T04, T07, T09, and T10;
- Skill-guided procedures in T04, T07, T09, and T10;
- registered research-intent and mathematical requests in T02, T05, T06, and PHS-2D;
- candidate, evidence, and decision links across the task portfolio.

These are trace-based component-use records. The component-specific ON/OFF designs are maintained separately and are not reported as completed outcome ablations unless corresponding controlled runs exist.

## Reporting rules

Use each task's frozen metrics and required-condition denominator. Keep T10 frozen controls separate. Do not average raw errors across task families. Keep the nine-segment PHS-2D result and its 18-pair gradient audit separate from older topology snapshots. Preserve the historical identity of C in all comparisons.
